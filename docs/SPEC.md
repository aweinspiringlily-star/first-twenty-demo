# First Twenty: product spec

Synthesized by a judge agent from three independent design proposals (founder, first people-ops hire, hiring manager), October 2026. This is the build target. Where it conflicts with ARCHITECTURE.md conventions, ARCHITECTURE.md wins on conventions and this file wins on product behavior.

## How the proposals were weighed

Rubric: each proposal scored 1 to 5 on fit for 1 to 20 people, lightweight, California legal correctness, and lifecycle completeness.

FOUNDER: 5/4/5/5 = 19, and the base for this spec.
- Best fit: a 6-stage pipeline, because the founder is both recruiter and hiring manager until about hire 10; the First hire kit; onboarding and time off folded into People and the inbox; only two hard stops.
- Most precise law: the 20-calendar-week federal test, contractors counting toward the harassment threshold, the on-site count for SB 553, ADEA 21/7 releases without storing age, 25102(o), 409A, credit-check limits, SB 294, and dropping 26.
- Weaknesses: optional power features crept into must-haves; the 2810.5 notice went to everyone; no SOC 2 evidence pack, no DE 542, no pay-change workflow; a sample with no departures.

PEOPLE OPS: 4/3/4/5 = 16.
- Strongest system-of-record discipline: one versioned storage key, audit log, SOC 2 evidence pack, DE 542 and W-9, SB 700, 2810.5 limited to non-exempt hires, rates with sources, a change job or pay workflow, a former employee in the sample, the First twenty wall.
- Too heavy: 10 nav destinations, pay zones, plan scenarios and a 7-stage pipeline.
- Legal slips: federal laws said to apply at 15 without the 20-week test; I-9 days counted against federal holidays rather than days the business is open; the 2025 San Jose minimum wage.

HIRING: 3/2/3/4 = 12.
- Best ATS craft: the threshold preview on Hire, small-n honesty, salary-history lint, scorecard due windows, dual time zones, back-scheduling.
- Wrong for a company making about one hire a month: availability grids, a question bank, an interviewer load meter, resumes in IndexedDB, illustrative equity values.
- Legal slips: said contractors never count, and that wage-history records start at 15 (they apply at any size).

GRAFTED from people ops: SOC 2 evidence; DE 542 (individuals only) and W-9; SB 700; 2810.5 for non-exempt hires only; the change job or pay workflow; versioned storage, with the audit log merged into Activity; Rules and rates with sources; red reserved for statutory deadlines; weekend-aware due dates; 'Data we don't keep'; the board print; an alumnus in the sample (Ben Carter, whose exit explains the backfill job); the First twenty wall as a Directory view.

GRAFTED from hiring: the threshold preview on Hire, salary-history lint, scorecards due in 24 hours, dual time zones, small-n metrics and back-scheduling (should-haves), and referrals from Employee view.

CUT: Triage survives only as a Sweep mode of the pipeline list. Also cut: zones, scenarios, the question bank, the scheduler grid, the interviewer load meter, file storage, birthdays, illustrative equity value, and separate Time off, Onboarding and My team tabs.

LEGAL FIXES that no proposal had:
- Paid organ and bone marrow donor leave at 15.
- Lactation policy delivered at hire.
- Noncompete (B&P 16600) and Labor Code 925 guardrails in the offer letter.
- CalSavers firing at the first non-owner employee.
- Labor Code 1024.5 bases plus a written-notice task for credit checks.
- A $2,000 1099-NEC threshold for 2026 payments.
- San Jose's 2026 minimum wage set at $18.45 (flagged to verify).
- Harassment training required only for CA-based staff.
- Sick leave restored on rehire within 12 months.
- DE 2320 and the UI 1089 notice tied to discharge, layoff or leave.
- Leave types hidden from non-admins.
- The Fair Chance flow held to the 5+ standard at every size.
- Sample run-rate recomputed bottom-up (about $235K a month now).

CHANGES TO THE SKETCH: stages become New, Intro call, Technical, Onsite, Offer, Hired. 26 becomes 25, and 10-on-site, 75 and 100 are added. Time off and onboarding move under People. The headcount plan lives in Comp and plan but also appears as planned rows in Hiring.

## Navigation

### Home (home)

Tonight's HR work and where the company stands, on one screen. Inbox items carry inline actions, so most sessions never leave Home. Content changes with the Viewing as mode.

- Tonight header (item count and time estimate)
- Action inbox (Overdue, This week, Coming up; filter Waiting on you or Waiting on them)
- Next threshold card with headcount ruler and projected date
- Headcount vs plan step chart since founding
- This week (starts, interviews, who's out, cliffs and anniversaries, deadlines)
- Hiring pulse (open jobs, mini funnels, offers out)
- Onboarding and offboarding in flight
- People cost and runway (Admin only, blurs with B)
- First hire kit (replaces the cost, onboarding and hiring blocks while headcount is 1)

### Hiring (hiring)

Every job and every candidate, with a guaranteed next step for each person so nobody is dropped. California hiring rules are built into the forms. Plan lines show here as 'planned' rows, so opening a job takes one click.

- Jobs (open, planned, draft, on hold, filled; mini funnel, days open, stuck count, backfill flag)
- Job pipeline (list or board, Sweep mode, a button and a key for every drag)
- Candidates (search all; keep-warm, silver medalist and referral filters)
- Candidate profile (stage stepper, next step, timeline, scorecards, desired pay, links, offer, background check status)
- Offers (draft, awaiting approval, out with expiry, accepted, declined with reason)
- Interview kits (role templates: interview plan, scorecard attributes with anchors, email templates; 13 in the sample, 9 company-neutral starters after Start fresh)

### People (people)

Everyone who works here and what each person needs next. Onboarding, offboarding and time off live here and in the inbox, not in their own top-level sections.

- Directory (List, Org chart, First twenty wall; active, starting, on leave, alumni; employees, contractors, advisors)
- Profile tabs (Overview, Job and pay, Equity, Time off, Checklists and documents, Emergency contacts, Activity)
- Checklists (running onboarding, offboarding, leave and new-state checklists with due dates)
- Time off (who's out calendar, requests, balances, leave cases, holidays)
- Contractors and advisors (AB5 result, DE 542, W-9, contract dates, 1099 flag)

### Comp and plan (plan)

What we pay, what we've granted, who we hire next, what it costs and each payroll's prep. Headcount planning sits with comp because every plan line is a cost decision. Admin only.

- Headcount plan (role lines by quarter toward 20, linked to jobs and people; one-page board print)
- Levels and bands (IC1 to IC5 and M1 to M2 by track, AI premium, equity guidelines)
- Comp table (base, variable, band position, compa-ratio, last change, exception reason; blur)
- Equity (pool, grants and vesting, board consent queue, 409A history)
- Cost and runway (fully loaded now, after pending offers, at plan; contractors shown separately)
- Pay runs (each pay period prepared for the payroll provider or PEO: who is on it, what changed, estimated gross, checks, export, Mark submitted, provider totals)

### Compliance (compliance)

What the law expects at your size, in plain English, with one-tap tasks, official sources and the proof that you did it. Headcount thresholds, site counts, first-time events and calendar dates all trigger obligations. Admin only.

- Milestones (ruler 0 to 20 with faded 25 to 100; threshold, site and event cards, each with 'How we counted')
- Calendar (recurring and dated obligations, .ics download)
- Training tracker (grid of people by course)
- Notices and policies (required notices packet, handbook versions, acknowledgements)
- SOC 2 evidence (period picker, 5 people controls, gaps, CSV)
- Records and retention (retain-until dates, anonymize queue, records requests)

### Settings (settings)

One home per setting. Opinionated defaults ship filled in, so most founders never open it. Admin only. Display options live in the header so every view can reach them.

- Company and funding (legal name, entity, HQ, founded date, funding, cash on hand, loaded-cost assumptions)
- Departments, locations and levels
- Hiring (stages, SLAs, rejection reasons, email templates, Calendly link)
- Checklist templates (onboarding, offboarding, new state, leave)
- Time off policies and holidays
- Rules and rates (each value has a source link and a last-reviewed date)
- Link-outs (payroll or PEO, background check vendor, e-sign, cap table tool, training providers) and pay run settings (cutoff, weekend and holiday paydays, semi-monthly paydays)
- Data (backup, import with preview, reset sample, start fresh as a solo founder, storage meter, Data we don't keep)

### Me (me)

Self-service for one person: their own record, requests and tasks. It is the home screen in Employee view. In Admin or Hiring manager view it shows your own record.

- My summary (printable)
- My job and pay (pay history and the pay band for my position)
- My equity (vesting chart, next vest, cliff countdown, exercise window explained)
- My time off (request; vacation and CA sick balances shown separately; holidays)
- My tasks (new-hire items, trainings, policy acknowledgements)
- My profile (contact info, emergency contacts, SB 294 arrest-notification contact)
- Open roles (posted ranges, Refer someone)

### Global header (every view) (header)

The few controls that must be one tap away everywhere.

- + New (N): candidate, job, person, contractor, time off
- Search and command palette (/ or Ctrl+K)
- Viewing as switch with person picker and the 'Preview, not security' banner
- Backup-age chip (amber after 7 days)
- Privacy blur (B)
- Display menu (text size 100 to 150%, light or dark, reduced motion, shortcuts on or off)
- ? shortcut sheet with large keycaps, listing the keys the current screen offers (on a job pipeline: A, R, S, Enter and the arrows, still listed once the sheet takes focus from the board)
- Undo toast that names the change

## Must have

- PLATFORM: one self-contained HTML file with no server. Charts are hand-rolled SVG, with no heavy libraries. All state lives in one versioned localStorage key (firsttwenty:v1) with a schemaVersion and migrations. First paint takes under 1 second with the sample loaded, and JSON export and import round-trip without loss.
- HEADER: the global header appears on every view, exactly as listed in navigation.header. The command palette jumps to any person, candidate, job or setting, with 48px result rows.
- ACCESSIBILITY IS A BUILD GATE: 48px minimum targets with 8px gaps; visible focus and a skip link; every action reachable by keyboard; nothing drag-only or hover-only. Single-key shortcuts can be turned off and none needs a chord (/ opens the palette). Undo replaces confirm dialogs, and Undo stays available from the activity list after the toast fades. Dates come from chips (Today, Tomorrow, Next Mon, +1 week, +2 weeks, Pick) instead of typing, and every field has a default. WCAG AA contrast in light and dark, reduced motion, sentence-case copy, no em dashes, and a dictation hint (Win+H or macOS dictation) in notes boxes.
- CLICK BUDGETS are checked in QA: add a candidate in 2 actions, advance in 1, reject with reason and notify in 2, a scorecard in 5 taps, approve time off in 1, request time off in 3, go from accepted offer to running onboarding in 1, and mark a checklist item done in 1.
- ACTION INBOX is computed live from data and never typed. Sources: checklist items due or overdue, scorecards owed, candidates with no next step or past SLA, expiring offers, approvals (offers, requisitions, time off), grants awaiting board consent, AB5 risks, compliance tasks and calendar items, leave notices, and backup age. Each row has one 48px inline primary action, Snooze (1 day, 3 days, 1 week) and Undo. Statutory items cannot be snoozed past their due date; they can only be marked done, or not applicable with a note. Rows are grouped Overdue, This week and Coming up, with a Waiting on you or Waiting on them filter. Only statutory deadlines turn red.
- HOME blocks are exactly as listed in homeDashboard, with variants per view. While headcount is 1, a First hire kit replaces the cost, onboarding and hiring blocks.
- ROLE TEMPLATES: the sample company (Ledgerlark) has 13 templates for a financial AI startup; a company that starts fresh gets 9 company-neutral starters instead (senior full-stack engineer, machine learning engineer, product designer, product manager, founding account executive, customer success manager, solutions engineer, finance and accounting lead (CPA preferred), ops and people generalist), since a posting goes out with the template's text (src/data/starterRoleTemplates.ts). Each prefills level, band, posted pay range, JD outline, interview plan and 3 to 5 scorecard attributes, with one-line anchors and 2 suggested questions per attribute. Sample roles: senior full-stack engineer, ML engineer, applied AI engineer (evals), data engineer (ERP connectors), security and compliance engineer, product designer, product manager (close and reconciliation), founding account executive, solutions engineer, implementation lead, customer success manager (CPA preferred), accounting lead (CPA), ops and people generalist. Example attributes: LLM evals and error analysis; accounting judgment (accruals, reconciliations, flux); ERP integration design; security mindset; explaining agent output to a controller.
- JOBS LIST shows open, draft, on hold and filled jobs, plus greyed 'planned' rows from the headcount plan with a one-click Open job. Each row shows a mini funnel, days open, stuck count, next interview and hiring manager. A job links to its plan line and carries a Backfill flag when it replaces someone.
- STAGES: the default pipeline is New (applied or sourced), Intro call, Technical, Onsite, Offer, Hired. Rejected and Withdrew are outcomes with reasons, not columns. Stages can be renamed, added or removed per job, and Recruiter screen is an optional stage to add once there is a recruiter. SLAs are in business days: New 3, Intro call 5, Technical 5, Onsite 3, Offer until expiry.
- PIPELINE VIEW: list (the default under 768px) or board, plus Sweep mode. Sweep shows one candidate at a time with big Advance (A), Reject (R), Snooze 3 days (S) and Skip (J) buttons, takes under 10 seconds per candidate and ends with a summary. Every drag has a button and a key (Move to... picker). Cards show days in stage, colored against the SLA.
- REQUIRED NEXT STEP: every active candidate always has a next step, a due date and an owner. A candidate goes to the inbox if they have no next step, are past the stage SLA, or have waited more than 5 business days to hear back.
- CANDIDATES: quick-add by pasting a blob; name, email, phone, LinkedIn and GitHub are parsed out, and duplicates are caught by email or profile URL. Source chips are Inbound, Referral (pick referrer), Outbound and Agency. The profile shows a stage stepper, next step, timeline, quick-log buttons (Emailed, Called, Left voicemail), scorecards, desired pay, sponsorship needed (yes, no, unknown), links (never files), tags and a retain-until date.
- CLOSING THE LOOP: Reject or Withdrew needs one reason chip (not a fit for level, skills gap, chose another offer, comp, location, timing, no response) and a notify step. The notify step opens a stage-aware template in the mail app or copies it (kind or brief tone, founder signature); for final-round rejections it suggests a call instead. When a job fills, bulk close-out copies the BCC list and a close-out note. Closed candidates are archived, never deleted.
- SCORECARDS: 3 to 5 role attributes on a 4-point scale with no neutral, plus an overall Strong no, No, Yes or Strong yes. One takes under 30 seconds and notes are optional. Each is due 24 hours after the interview, with an inbox nudge. Scorecards stay hidden from other interviewers until they submit their own. Admin can enter one on an interviewer's behalf, logged as 'entered by'.
- SCHEDULING without integrations: a copyable message with 3 proposed times or the Calendly link, with times in Pacific and in the candidate's time zone, plus .ics downloads for the candidate and the panel. Scheduling an interview creates owed scorecards automatically.
- CA HIRING GUARDRAILS live in the forms. There is no salary-history field anywhere (desired pay only), and a gentle lint fires if a note mentions current or past salary. Every job has a pay range from day one: applicants can request it at any size, and Colorado requires it on postings a CO resident could fill. Copy posting text includes the range, a 'we don't ask about salary history' line, a Fair Chance statement (best practice statewide, required in LA and SF), the sponsorship stance and a benefits summary. No cannabis-use questions (SB 700). Criminal-history fields stay locked until a conditional offer exists, at every company size.
- EXACTLY TWO HARD STOPS: (1) once active employees plus accepted offers reach 15, a job cannot be marked posted without a pay range (Labor Code 432.3); (2) criminal history cannot be recorded before a conditional offer. Everything else is a warning with a why and a citation chip, except that an offer never goes out, and its letter is never generated, with pay its classification cannot carry (see OFFER BUILDER).
- BACKGROUND CHECKS AND FAIR CHANCE: a check starts only after Accepted records a conditional offer date. Consent and the FCRA and ICRAA disclosures go through a vendor link-out. The result is stored as Clear or Consider (status only, never the report). Consider opens the Fair Chance stepper: individualized assessment note, preliminary notice with the report and rights, a response clock of 5 business days (5 more if the candidate disputes), then final notice. Each step has a computed due date, and the offer is confirmed or rescinded with the reason logged. Credit checks are off by default. Turning one on for a job requires a Labor Code 1024.5 basis picked from a list (for example a managerial role, or authority to move company money or sign on its accounts) and creates a written-notice task (Civil Code 1785.20.5).
- OFFER BUILDER on one screen: level picks the band. Base defaults to the band midpoint, or to desired pay if that is inside the band, and snaps to $1K. Equity defaults to the level guideline and shows shares, % fully diluted and a strike estimate at the current 409A (internal only). Start date and a 5-business-day expiry are prefilled. A position-in-band meter shows desired pay and peers as unnamed dots. Going above band or guideline needs a one-tap exception reason. Exempt check: a base below the CA exempt floor ($70,304 for 2026, which is 2 x $16.90 x 2,080, editable yearly), or a computer-professional role below that hourly minimum, flags the role non-exempt and warns about overtime, meal and rest breaks, and timekeeping. An exempt offer below the floor, or pay below the minimum wage for the work location, blocks Mark sent and the letter until the pay or the classification is fixed; the blocker stays on the offer after Save. So does a start date or an expiry date that has passed: Mark sent and the letter wait for a new start date, Extend moves the start along with the expiry ('Extend to <date>, start <date>'), and Accepted and Hire still work late (see ONE-CLICK HIRE). The builder also checks the job's posted pay range. Employment type (full time, part time, intern) and hours a week (whole or half hours, 1 to 60) are set on the job and the offer; a part-time letter says part-time with the hours. Until the cap table is confirmed in Comp and plan > Equity, the % fully diluted is marked as an estimate from placeholder numbers. Approval routes to Admin only when someone else drafted the offer, and approving is one tap in the inbox. A manager's pay, equity, level or start-date edit to an Admin draft needs Admin approval again. Declined records the reason and, by default, also closes the application as withdrawn; clearing 'Also close the application' keeps it open with the next step 'Decide: make a new offer or close out'. Backing out after accepting always closes it. An expired offer offers Extend and Declined.
- GENERATED DOCUMENTS: print to PDF, e-sign by link-out, tracked as status and date. Offer letter: at-will, contingencies, equity subject to board approval with shares and % fully diluted (shares only while the cap table holds Start fresh's placeholder numbers), strike set at grant, no dollar projections, no noncompete (Business and Professions Code 16600), and CA law and venue (Labor Code 925). Labor Code 2810.5 wage notice for non-exempt hires (optional for exempt). PIIA reminder that includes the Labor Code 2870 notice.
- ONE-CLICK HIRE from an accepted offer. It first previews the threshold impact ('This hire takes you to 15: 2 things change'). It then creates employee #N as Pre-start, with effective-dated job and comp records, a grant queued for board consent at the current 409A, a filled plan line (a new filled line for a job outside the plan), a referral bonus date if the hire was referred, an onboarding checklist with computed dates, and a payroll task for a sign-on bonus the letter promised. The work email is built on the company domain (the website, or the founder's work email); with neither it stays empty for Admin to set. A former employee hired again is linked to the earlier record (sick leave left at exit comes back within 12 months). Hire fills the job's plan line only while nobody holds it: a second hire from the same job keeps the first hire on their line and gets a filled line of their own, and the offer builder and the Hire preview say it adds a seat outside the plan. When the start date has passed by the time the offer is accepted or hired, the offer asks whether they started then: 'Yes, they started on <date>' keeps the date (they count as active at once, with first-day tasks such as I-9 section 1 already due), or Move start sets the next business day. Finally it offers close-out notes for the remaining candidates.
- DIRECTORY has three views. List: active, starting soon, on leave and alumni, across employees, contractors and advisors. Org chart: a keyboard-navigable tree built from the manager field, with planned roles as dashed boxes. First twenty wall: employee numbers in hire order; alumni stay as outlined seats and empty seats show the planned role and quarter.
- PROFILE TABS: Overview; Job and pay (effective-dated title, level, manager, FLSA class, base, variable, reason and approver, which also covers the job title and wage rate history CA requires at any size); Equity (grants, vesting chart, next vest, cliff); Time off; Checklists and documents (status, date and a link to where the file lives, never the file); Emergency contacts (with the SB 294 arrest-notification choice); Activity.
- CHANGE JOB OR PAY from the profile: pick a reason chip (promotion, market adjustment, merit, title only, manager change). The new level shows band position before and after, and the effective date defaults to the next pay period. The exempt check reruns, the approval is recorded, history rows are written, an optional refresh grant is queued for board consent, and an 'Update payroll by <date>' task is created. Someone's first direct report assigns the 2-hour supervisor harassment training, due within 6 months. A change for a non-exempt employee creates a 2810.5 change-notice task due in 7 days. The preview lists only the tasks the save creates. Changes still to take effect show in a Scheduled callout, one row each with what it changes; Admin can cancel one before it takes effect (the latest first), which removes its history row and the checklist, supervisor training and refresh grant only it created, with Undo.
- ONBOARDING TEMPLATE (CA-aware): business-day math skips weekends and company holidays, and labels are weekend-aware ('due Sun Nov 15, file by Fri Nov 13'). Pre-start: PIIA, background check cleared, payroll or PEO invite, laptop with MDM and disk encryption, accounts with MFA and least privilege, welcome email. Day 1: I-9 section 1, W-4 (2026) and DE 4 (W-4 and the work state's own form outside CA), required notices packet, handbook and security policy acknowledgements, emergency contacts. Business day 3: I-9 section 2. Day 20: confirm DE 34 filed. Day 30: security awareness training, production-access approval and 30-day check-in (SOC 2 evidence). Day 90: check-in. Month 6: harassment training (CA-based staff; 2 hours for supervisors). Equity: grant in the next board consent, and 83(b) within 30 days of an early exercise, with no extension.
- CONDITIONAL CHECKLIST ITEMS keep templates short. An item is included only when the company has 5+ employees, or the person supervises, is non-exempt, is remote, works outside CA (this adds the shallow new-state card), has equity, or early-exercises.
- REQUIRED NOTICES PACKET at hire, each item marked delivered with a date: CRD sexual harassment pamphlet; workers' comp pamphlet and personal physician predesignation form; DE 2515 (SDI); DE 2511 (PFL); ACA marketplace notice (within 14 days); SB 294 workplace know-your-rights notice with the optional arrest-notification contact; lactation accommodation policy (Labor Code 1034); 2810.5 notice for non-exempt hires; CFRA and PDL notices at 5+; handbook acknowledgement.
- OFFBOARDING WIZARD: pick the type (resigned with 72+ hours notice, resigned without notice, terminated, laid off, contract ended) and the last day. The app shows the final pay deadline under Labor Code 201 to 203: at separation for a termination or layoff, on the last day for a resignation with notice, within 72 hours without notice. It warns about waiting-time penalties and shows the payout rule: accrued vacation is paid, flexible PTO pays nothing if truly unlimited, sick leave is never paid. Checklist: change-in-relationship notice (UI Code 1089) and DE 2320 on discharge, layoff or leave (optional on resignation); COBRA or Cal-COBRA via the PEO or carrier (for Cal-COBRA, tell the carrier within 31 days) and the HIPP notice; access revoked the same day with an evidence link; laptop return, never deducted from final pay; reports reassigned; vested shares and the 90-day exercise deadline in the exit note; rehire eligible yes or no. The status becomes Alumni and retention dates are set. The exit cancels job and pay changes scheduled after the last day, cancels time off after it (or shortens it to end there) and closes tasks due after it, and the summary says what it cancelled. 'Backfill?' reopens the plan line as a job in one tap. REHIRE: an alumni profile offers Rehire, which opens Add person prefilled and linked to the earlier record; Add person also spots a likely former employee and offers to link them.
- CONTRACTORS AND ADVISORS live in People with a worker type. AB5 check: an exemption picker (business-to-business; professional services such as graphic design or marketing; none), then prongs A, B and C as yes or no with examples. The result reads likely OK, review, or likely employee, with plain reasons and an 'ask counsel' prompt on review or likely employee. Likely employee offers a prefilled Convert to employee offer or an end-by date. Also tracked: rate, hours, contract dates, W-9 on file, DE 542 within 20 days of paying or contracting for $600+ (individual contractors only, not LLCs or corporations), a 1099-NEC flag with a Jan 31 reminder (IRS threshold editable; $2,000 for 2026 payments), and end-date reminders. Contractors count toward the 5-person harassment training threshold but never toward headcount. Advisors carry NSO grants and no checklist.
- TIME OFF: a request takes 3 taps (type chip, date chips, submit), and workdays are counted skipping weekends and holidays. Approval is one tap from the inbox. The default is flexible vacation for exempt staff; accrued vacation is optional, with a cap and a payout-at-exit warning. CA paid sick leave is tracked separately: 40 hours or 5 days front-loaded each Jan 1 and at hire, an optional 90-day wait before use, no reason asked, auto-approved, never paid out, and restored if the person is rehired within 12 months. Sick leave asked for before the wait ends is held for Admin, and the Time off tab and the inbox offer the same choice: Waive the wait (paid as sick leave) or Record as unpaid, never a plain Approve. Before the start date the panel says what day 1 brings, restored hours included. At 5+ employees: bereavement (5 days) and reproductive loss (5 days). At 15+: organ donor (up to 30 business days) and bone marrow donor (up to 5 business days) paid leave. At any size: jury duty and voting time. Includes 2026 and 2027 holiday sets, a who's-out calendar and .ics for approved time off.
- LEAVE CASES for CFRA, PDL and bonding: start date, expected return, and an eligibility hint (CFRA needs 12 months of service and 1,250 hours in the last 12 months; PDL has no service requirement). A response and designation notice is due within 5 business days of the request. Each case adds a return-to-work task and an EDD link for SDI and PFL. No medical details are stored, and non-admins see only 'Leave, back <date>' or 'Out'.
- LEVELS AND BANDS: levels IC1 to IC5 and M1 to M2 by track (engineering, product and design, go-to-market, finance and ops). Each level has band min, mid and max, an AI premium % for ML and AI roles, an equity guideline as % fully diluted, a variable target or OTE split, and an FLSA default. There is one band set, anchored to San Jose. The comp table shows base, variable, a band position dot, compa-ratio, last change and exception reason. Band edits are logged.
- EQUITY PLANNING VIEW (the cap table tool stays the source of truth): pool granted, awaiting board, reserved by offers and available. Grants vest over 4 years with a 1-year cliff then monthly, as ISOs for employees; contractors and advisors get NSOs, and advisors vest over 2 years monthly. Shows upcoming cliffs and 409A history, with a 60-day expiry warning and a warning if a financing closed since the last 409A. Board consent queue: Copy consent table (name, shares, type, strike, vest start, schedule), then Mark approved with the board date. The first grant ever creates a 25102(o) notice task due in 30 days. Record 409A (effective date, price a share, provider, safe harbor until, 12 months by default) and correct or remove one; Edit cap table (authorized, fully diluted, founder and pool shares). Start fresh fills in placeholder cap table numbers: until they are changed or confirmed ('These numbers are right'), Equity says so and offer letters give the share count without the %.
- HEADCOUNT PLAN: role lines by quarter toward 20, each with level, target start, budgeted base, loaded cost, status (planned, open, offer, filled, cut) and a backfill flag. Open job takes one click. Statuses roll up from the linked job and person, and budgeted vs offered variance is shown. A one-page board print covers actual vs plan chart, hires, open jobs, offers out, and cost now and at plan.
- COST AND RUNWAY: fully loaded monthly run-rate now, after pending offers, and at plan. Assumptions are editable: employer Social Security up to the $184,500 wage base, Medicare 1.45%, FUTA plus the CA credit reduction, CA UI and ETT on the first $7,000, benefits per head, PEO and tools per head, and workers' comp %. Contractors are shown separately. Runway comes from cash on hand and other monthly spend.
- PAY RUN PREP (Comp and plan > Pay runs, `#comp.payruns[.<period start>]`, Admin only): payroll stays with the provider or PEO, which calculates taxes, files, issues pay stubs and moves the money; First Twenty prepares each pay run. ## Growth journey

Principle: features appear when the data needs them, never by plan tier or permission. Anything can be switched on early in Settings, and each newly unlocked area announces itself once in the inbox with a one-line 'why now'. Headcount means W-2 employees anywhere, part-time included, with at least one in California; each rule states its own count.

SOLO (1, via Start fresh): 3 setup screens. Home shows the First hire kit: EDD account (DE 1 within 15 days of first paying over $100 in a quarter, even if the only W-2 is you), workers' comp before the first non-owner hire, IIPP, sick leave policy, federal, CA and San Jose posters, payroll or PEO link, offer and PIIA templates reviewed by counsel, option pool and first 409A. Hiring and Comp and plan work fully. People shows an empty state that teaches.

FIRST HIRE (2): onboarding with I-9 and DE 34 dates, the notices packet, CalSavers or a 401(k) (owner-only companies are exempt, so this fires now), the directory, time off, the run-rate card and Employee view. The first grant unlocks Equity (409A tracker, board consent queue, 25102(o) notice within 30 days).

2 TO 4: the comp table fills in. The first contractor triggers the AB5 check and DE 542 (individuals only). The first remote or out-of-state employee triggers Labor Code 2802 reimbursement, electronic posting and the shallow new-state card. The first non-exempt hire triggers the 2810.5 notice and the overtime, meal and rest break and timekeeping warnings.

5 (the big California step): FEHA, the Fair Chance Act (the app has enforced its gating since day one), CFRA and PDL, bereavement and reproductive loss leave, a written harassment policy, harassment training auto-assigned to CA-based staff (contractors count toward the 5), SB 700, and a nudge to set levels and bands before the next offer.

FIRST NON-FOUNDER MANAGER (usually hire 5 to 10): the Hiring manager view appears, time off and drafted offers route through managers, supervisor training is due within 6 months, and the org chart gets depth.

10 ON SITE AT ONCE (a site count, not headcount): a workplace violence prevention plan under SB 553, annual training and an incident log; remote staff are excluded. Around 10, an ops or people hire becomes a second admin, which prompts file autosave, the passphrase lock and a written handoff. The SOC 2 evidence pack becomes prominent when SOC 2 is on.

15: pay ranges become a hard stop on posts (counting accepted offers), and paid organ and bone marrow donor leave applies. The federal counter starts for Title VII, ADA, GINA and PWFA (15+ in each of 20 calendar weeks in the current or prior year); posters and an accommodation process are due when it completes.

20: the ADEA counter starts (20 for 20 weeks). Releases default to 21 days to review and 7 to revoke for everyone, so age is never needed. Federal COBRA applies from the next calendar year if there were 20+ employees on more than half of typical business days. A Graduation panel offers export packs and an honest note that past about 25 people, or with several hires in another state, a real HRIS and ATS fits better.

UPCOMING (faded on the ruler): 25 (school activities leave at one location, rehab and literacy accommodation, victims' leave notice); 36 (San Jose Opportunity to Work Ordinance: offer hours to existing part-timers before hiring); 50 (FMLA, ACA employer mandate); 75 (Cal-WARN); 100 (EEO-1, CA pay data report, federal WARN). Non-headcount triggers: revenue above the CCPA threshold (workforce privacy notice), first visa sponsorship (LCA posting), first group health plan. The sketch's 26 is dropped because the 26+ minimum wage tier ended in 2023.

## Viewing as rules

- It is a preview, not security. Every non-admin view shows a persistent banner: 'Preview, not security. Anyone using this browser can switch views.' Switching is one tap in the header, with a person picker, and the choice is remembered per browser. Every change records the actor and the viewing-as mode in Activity. Approvals made in a preview are labeled, for example 'recorded as Lena Park (preview)', so the record stays honest.
- Admin (founder or people ops) sees and can do everything: all six sections plus Me; every person's pay, variable, equity, cost and runway; all candidates, desired pay, notes and offers; scorecards (after submitting their own if they were on the panel); background check status and Fair Chance steps; leave types; Compliance, SOC 2 evidence, Settings and Data. Admin approves offers, requisitions, grants and time off. Only Admin can export, import, reset the sample or start fresh, and only Admin sees the people-cost block on Home.
- Hiring manager (pick a person) is offered once someone other than the founder has a direct report or is hiring manager on a job; in the sample that is Lena, Tomás and Grace. Nav is Home, Hiring, People and Me. Home shows only their inbox (scorecards they owe, decisions on candidates in their jobs, time off from direct reports, manager-owned onboarding items for their new hires, their team's overdue trainings, their requisition requests), their week and their jobs' pulse. No cost, compliance or threshold card.
- Hiring manager in Hiring. On jobs where they are hiring manager: the full pipeline, desired pay, and all scorecards once their own is in; they can advance, reject and draft offers inside the band, and drafts route to Admin for approval. On jobs where they are only on the panel: their own interviews and scorecards, with no desired pay, no offer, and no other scorecards until they submit theirs. Background checks show only 'contingencies cleared: yes or no'. Planned rows for their department show title, quarter and status, never loaded cost. They can request a new job, which lands as an approval in Admin's inbox.
- Hiring manager in People: the full directory and org chart. For their reports (and their reports' reports) they also see job and title history, time off balances and approvals, checklist and training status, and the pay band for each report's level. They never see individual pay, variable or equity for anyone, and leave shows as 'Leave, back Jan 25' with no type. No Comp and plan, Compliance or Settings.
- Employee (pick any person; defaults to the newest hire, Jasmine Torres): nav is Me and People. Me shows only their own record: profile (they edit contact info, emergency contacts and the SB 294 arrest-notification contact in place); job and title history; pay history and the pay band for their current position, which covers the CA right to request it; equity grants with vesting chart, next vest, cliff countdown and the exercise window explained; vacation and CA sick balances shown separately, with Request; their own tasks (new-hire items, trainings, policy acknowledgements); policies and holidays; and open roles with posted ranges plus Refer someone, which lands in Hiring as a referral.
- Employee in People: the directory and org chart (name, title, team, manager, location, start date, work anniversary) and a who's-out strip that says only 'Out'. - The Display menu (text size, theme, motion, shortcuts) and the ? shortcut sheet are available in every view.
